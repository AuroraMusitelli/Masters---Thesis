**📄Master's Thesis Time Series: Evaluating the predictive ability of BigVAR models, an application to energy consumption and CO₂ emissions in the United States**

This thesis analyzes the predictive capacity of high-dimensional BigVAR models that have been applied to multivariate time series concerning the energy-environmental system of the United States in the period 
1973-2025.
